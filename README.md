Your videos, images, and fonts stay on your device. No installation or account required.

## ✨ What you can do

- **🔍 Find loops** anywhere in a video, or within a range you choose. Compare matches and get a suggested loop method.
- **🔁 Shape the loop** with a natural cut, dissolve, forward-and-backward playback, or fade.
- **🩹 Repair small defects** with pause trimming, brightness correction, seam alignment, or static and moving patches.
- **🎨 Add layers** with text, images, gradients, custom fonts, movement, and rotation.
- **✂️ Frame your video** in portrait, landscape, square, or custom dimensions. Move, resize, and rotate it in the preview.
- **📱 Prepare iPhone wallpapers** and Spotify Canvas visuals, or export a regular video or GIF.

## 🚀 Make your first loop

1. **Open a video.** Click **Open media** or drag your file into the editor. A sample is already available when you first open the site.
2. **Choose a range.** Adjust the timeline, or use **Auto find** and click **Use this loop** on a match.
3. **Make it yours.** Set dimensions and audio in **Video settings**. Open **Layers** to add text or images, or **Repair loop** to improve a seam.
4. **Check and download.** Press **Play** in **Result** to update and play the loop. Compare with **Original**, watch a few repeats, then **Export**.

When moving, scaling, or rotating the video, the viewer stays in **Original**. Choose **Result** when you are ready to check the processed loop.

## 💾 Choose your download

| Format | Good for |
| --- | --- |
| **MP4** | Sharing videos and Spotify Canvas. |
| **WebM** | Video for websites. |
| **GIF** | Silent animations, played once or looped forever. |
| **Live Photo / JPG** | Animated or still iPhone wallpaper preparation. |

For Spotify Canvas, the finished video must be **3–8 seconds, portrait, and silent**. Live Photo import and Lock Screen animation depend on your device and iOS; see the [iPhone guide](./docs/iphone-wallpapers.md).

## 💡 Before you export

- **Check the join.** Search scores help compare candidates; watch the result to judge smoothness.
- **Check the duration.** A dissolve shortens the cycle; forward-and-backward playback makes it longer.
- **Check the audio.** Muting playback does not remove audio from the download — set export audio in **Video settings**.
